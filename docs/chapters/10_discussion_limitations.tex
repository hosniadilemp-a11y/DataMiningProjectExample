\chapter{Critical Discussion, Limitations \& Ethics}

\section{Synthesis of Scientific Discoveries}
Combining Exploratory Data Analysis, non-parametric statistical hypothesis testing, and multi-model supervised classification reveals coherent clinical insights:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Chronotropic Incompetence:} Blunted maximum exercise heart rate ($d = 1.34$, $p < 0.0001$) is the single strongest negative clinical indicator.
    \item \textbf{ST Segment Depression:} Exercise ST depression ($r = 0.42$, $p < 0.0001$) serves as the primary continuous ischemic marker.
    \item \textbf{Classifier Superiority:} Radial Basis Function (RBF) Support Vector Machine achieved optimal margin separation ($89.17\%$ Accuracy, $0.9422$ ROC-AUC), outperforming linear and tree-based ensembles.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Study Limitations}
A rigorous scientific evaluation must acknowledge potential study limitations:

\subsection{1. Cross-Sectional Data Limitation}
The dataset is cross-sectional. Observed statistical associations cannot prove clinical causality. Longitudinal prospective clinical trials are required to establish causal pathways.

\subsection{2. Class Imbalance Impact}
Although $30.3\%$ prevalence represents moderate imbalance ($2.3:1$), model recall ($76.70\%$) leaves $23.3\%$ of diseased patients unflagged. In clinical triage, false negatives carry high risk.

\subsection{3. Synthetic / Tabular Data Artifacts}
The Kaggle dataset reflects simulated clinical distributions. Real-world Electronic Health Records (EHR) exhibit higher noise, unstandardized units, missing values, and selection bias.

\section{Ethical Considerations \& Algorithmic Bias}
Deploying predictive models in healthcare introduces critical ethical considerations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Demographic Bias:} Models must be evaluated for equalized odds across age brackets and gender groups to prevent health disparities.
    \item \textbf{Automation Bias:} Clinicians must not defer blindly to algorithmic recommendations.
    \item \textbf{Data Privacy:} Patient vitals and medical data must comply with HIPAA / GDPR encryption standards.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future Technical Work}
Future research directions include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Tabular Deep Learning:} Evaluating deep architectures such as TabNet and FT-Transformers.
    \item \textbf{Explainable AI (XAI):} Integrating SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME for local feature attribution.
    \item \textbf{Wearable Time-Series Integration:} Extending pipelines to ingest continuous time-series streams from consumer smartwatches.
    \item \textbf{Federated Learning:} Deploying privacy-preserving federated training across multi-hospital networks.
\end{enumerate}
